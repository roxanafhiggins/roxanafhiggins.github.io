Built to spec, with three deviations

Every token, size, weight and offset in case-study-card-spec.md is used verbatim. Three things could not be followed as written.

  • The en dash. The spec asks for the years joined by an en dash, and names that character explicitly. That collides with the standing no dashes rule, so the years read 2022/23. Say the word and it becomes the dash, or "2022 to 23".
  • Two of three assets are missing. case-study-phones-workwise.jpg and case-study-card-reference.png were not supplied. The phone column uses the existing workwise-3-phones.webp, which has a transparent background rather than a baked pink glow, and a different aspect, so it renders 580×471 instead of 580×424. case-study-logo-workwise.png is Workwise's own mark pulled from their site, but only 32px, against the 96px the spec expects.
  • This is a preview page, not the live card. Direction A uses Figtree and a warm orange, blue and purple palette. The homepage runs Instrument Serif, Geist and Ocean Serenity teal, and has a dark theme. Dropping this in would leave card one looking like a different website from cards two and three, and the phone image only works on white so it would break in dark mode.

Folding it into the homepage is quick once you have picked a direction. It would also mean re-deleting the Scope, Research and Delivery rows, which carry copy written two rounds ago, and re-attaching the German strings that card now holds.

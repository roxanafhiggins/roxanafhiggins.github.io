Is this a fit?

I’d rather tell you now than three weeks in.

This is for you if…

  • ✓ People sign up for your product and stop partway through
  • ✓ You’ve already tried copy tweaks and reminders, and the number didn’t move
  • ✓ You want research and design from one person, not a handoff between three
  • ✓ You’d rather understand why people leave than guess at what to change
  • ✓ You want to keep the system running after the engagement ends

This is not for you if…

  • ✕ You need pixel execution against a spec someone else already wrote
  • ✕ You want a visual refresh without touching the flow underneath
  • ✕ There’s no way to reach your users, at all
  • ✕ You need a full-time designer rather than a focused engagement
  • ✕ The decision is already made and you want it validated
